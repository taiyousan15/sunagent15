### F2 (MED) — Catalog comparison deferred — **scope reduction INAPPROPRIATE**
The `--strict` suffix pattern (`-report`, `-summary`, `-log`) cannot detect:

### F3 (MED) — slug regex — **STILL OPEN**

- Trailing hyphen (`abc-`) is allowed
- Consecutive hyphens (`a--b`) are allowed

A denylist + tighter pattern is needed.
